2. The problem
A changed prompt can improve some answers while breaking others. EvalAxis brings examples, checks and human judgement into one review workflow.
Do not invent market statistics or claim validated demand.

3. How it works
Step 1: Start with synthetic examples or upload a CSV of test cases and responses.
Step 2: Compare required phrases, forbidden phrases, word limits, JSON formatting and source markers.
Step 3: Inspect regressions and critical cases, then record a human Pass/Fail assessment with a reason.
Step 4: Export a readable report and structured evidence.

Public beta: prepared examples, CSV import, A/B comparison, explainable rule checks, human reviews and HTML/JSON/CSV exports.
